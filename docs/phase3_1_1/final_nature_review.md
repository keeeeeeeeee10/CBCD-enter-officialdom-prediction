## Overall assessment

The final package supports its bounded central claim. The manuscript consistently distinguishes latent true entry (T), observed ENTRY-record presence (E), and observed posting-record presence (P). It identifies documentation and selection processes as part of the predictive signal and avoids converting SHAP or grouped ablations into causal statements. The frozen evidence and reported uncertainty agree across prose, tables, figures, and audit artifacts.

The work is most relevant to computational historians, prosopography researchers, digital-humanities data curators, and researchers studying prediction from selected administrative or archival records. Its broader methodological value is the explicit separation between a database label and a latent historical construct.
